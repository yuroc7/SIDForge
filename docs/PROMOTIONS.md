# SID Forge promotions and licensing

SID Forge licensing is account-based. Current plans and prices are published at [sidforge.pp.ua/pricing](https://sidforge.pp.ua/pricing).

## Available access types

Depending on the current offer, customers can choose:

- one SID change;
- an operation pack for several computers;
- monthly access;
- annual access.

## Promotional codes

An active promotional code may grant a defined number of operations, supported devices, or a limited access period. The customer account shows the exact allowance and expiration after successful redemption.

Promotions are optional campaigns, not a permanent free edition. Availability, limits, and eligibility may change. Use only codes published by SID Forge or provided directly by an authorized SID Forge administrator.

Never publish account credentials, activation requests, diagnostic archives, or private promotion codes in a public issue.
